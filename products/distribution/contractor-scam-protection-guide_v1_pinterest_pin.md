Watch for these contractor fraud red flags before signing anything: asks for 50%+ upfront, refuses checks or credit cards, can't produce a valid license number, or offers "today-only" deals. This guide walks you through the exact verification steps that take 15 minutes and could save your entire deposit. Includes the payment structure that protects homeowners, contract clauses that matter most, and what to do if you've already lost money to a scam. Don't let urgency push you into a costly mistake.
