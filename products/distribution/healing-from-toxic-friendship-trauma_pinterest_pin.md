## Description
You know what happened was real, even when others don't get it. This free guide walks you through exactly how to recover from friendship trauma — from rebuilding your judgment and boundaries to stopping the 2am spirals. You'll learn why your body still braces for impact, how to trust your own memory again, and what to do with the anger you're finally letting yourself feel. No generic advice. No "just move on." A structured path forward for people who were damaged, not just disappointed.
