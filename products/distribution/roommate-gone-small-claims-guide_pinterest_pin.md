You split rent. You kept your end of the deal. Now your roommate is ghosting on their share and you're covering their portion. Includes evidence collection templates, state-specific filing instructions, and real-world enforcement strategies. Perfect for anyone dealing with roommate debt, rental disputes, or someone who's decided spite matters more than their word.
