## Description
OCD spiraling while waiting for medical results is a specific kind of hell. You don't need to be told to breathe or relax. You need actual tools that interrupt spirals without reassurance-seeking that makes anxiety worse. This guide gives OCD and health anxiety patients concrete coping frameworks for waiting periods—from crisis interruption to functioning when your brain is convinced functioning is dangerous. Includes: how to interrupt spirals without feeding them, how to tolerate uncertainty without safety behaviors that backfire, how to function at baseline while waiting. If your health anxiety makes medical waiting feel unbearable, these frameworks were built for exactly this.
