## Notes
**Visual direction:** Dark background with bold white or red text — strong contrast for readability in mobile grid. Use imagery like a crossed-out dollar sign or a gavel with money, or a frustrated homeowner silhouette. Keep text large and centered. Avoid stock photos of smiling contractors — this audience is angry and looking for answers, not feel-good imagery.

**Content focus:** Lead with the emotional pain point (deposit taken, contractor gone) in the visual and title, then promise a concrete outcome in the description. The description should emphasize "no lawyer needed" and "step-by-step" — these are the two biggest hesitations this audience has.

**Testing angle for Alt pin:** Try "skip out" language vs "ghosted" language across variants to see which pulls higher click-through from the home improvement audience.
