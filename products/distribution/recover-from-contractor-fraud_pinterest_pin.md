## Description
Before you stop payment or hire someone new, you need to know exactly what was contracted versus what's actually complete. This scope audit gives you the documented comparison that forms the foundation for stop-payment notices, liens, and recovery claims. Learn the exact line-by-line process to document substandard work, incomplete phases, and materials left on-site. Without this, you're shooting blind—risking payments for work not done or losing leverage for recovery. This is the first tactical move that changes your position from reactive to in-control.
