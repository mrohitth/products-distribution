**Title:** "If your kitten is driving you up the wall at 8 months old, this is normal (and here's what actually helps)"

**Body:**

Eight months is peak chaos for a lot of kittens. The hunting instincts are fully online. The impulse control is nonexistent. And your hands look a lot like prey.

You're not alone in this. A lot of people in this sub have been where you are — scratched up, sleep-deprived, wondering if they'll ever have a calm cat.

Here's what's helped a lot of us get through it:

**The hunting sequence game.** Five to ten minutes of structured play (stalk → chase → pounce → capture → eat) drains more energy than thirty minutes of random wrestling with a toy. Run it before your kitten's energy peaks and you'll often skip the ambush entirely.

**The yelp technique, done correctly.** One short, high-pitched "eek!" within half a second of a bite. Then stand up and walk away. No drama. The timing is everything — half a second or it doesn't register.

**Energy audit.** Track your kitten's activity for 48 hours. You'll find the peaks. Schedule intense play right before them.
