## Reddit Bridge Post

**Target subreddit:** r/homeowners

Title: I spent $1,800 on an emergency HVAC repair. The $12 filter I never replaced would have prevented it.

Body:

Six months ago, my AC stopped working at 2 AM on a Saturday in July. The technician showed up at 7 AM, diagnosed a failed compressor, and quoted me $1,800 for the repair.

I paid it. I had no choice.

The compressor failed because the condenser coil was dirty and the refrigerant pressure had been building for months. The dirty coil was the result of never hosing it off in three years of homeownership.

I did not know that was a thing.

I did not grow up with a house. Nobody taught me that HVAC systems need routine maintenance. I thought "if it ain't broke, don't fix it" was the right approach. It is not.

The math: a new compressor costs $1,200–$2,500. Hosing off the condenser coil twice a year is free. Replacing a $12 air filter every 90 days costs about $30 a year.

After that emergency, I learned what I should have known from day one. I put together a complete guide with:
- The maintenance tasks that prevent catastrophic failures ($0–$50/year)
- The negotiation framework for service calls (get diagnostics in writing, get second quotes, ask what the technician would do if it were their house)
- The repair vs. replace decision framework (the 50% rule, age thresholds, efficiency gains from replacement)

It is free to download. There is a checklist that you can stick on your furnace and use as a maintenance reminder every season.
