## Description (keyword-rich)
She's mentally finished with the marriage. You're still buried in paperwork, custody negotiations, and court dates. That gap between her "done" mindset and your legal reality is where fathers lose ground—and most divorce advice ignores it entirely.

This guide breaks down what's actually happening when your ex has emotionally checked out of the process, why that makes everything move slower for you specifically, and the exact steps to push forward without destroying your coparenting relationship. You'll learn how to communicate so she engages, when legal leverage helps versus when it's expensive noise, and how to stay present as a parent with full standing—not a visitor with supervised time.
