This guide walks you through the entire process: classifying your funding type, identifying which dollars are actually taxable, calculating and making quarterly payments, filing the right forms, and handling state taxes. Stop guessing at midnight on April 14th. Get the step-by-step system grad students actually need to stay compliant without the panic.
