## Description
If you've ever spent the night Googling symptoms while waiting for medical results, you know how quickly the brain goes from "probably nothing" to "terminal diagnosis by morning." This is your nervous system doing exactly what it was designed to do — filling silence with worst-case scenarios.

The problem isn't your thinking. It's your body.

This guide gives you nervous-system-aware tools that actually work in the moment: grounding techniques to pull yourself back to now, regulation strategies for when your hands won't stop shaking, and ways to interrupt the catastrophic thinking loop before it hijacks you. You'll learn why affirmations fail and what your nervous system actually needs during medical wait times.

Built for anyone facing medical uncertainty — biopsies, scans, callbacks, referrals. Real tools for the 2am spiral.
