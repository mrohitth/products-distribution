# Product Skeleton: Cat Aggression Medical vs. Behavioral Guide

---

## 1. Product Title

**Title (39 chars):** Why Is My Cat Biting Me All of a Sudden?

This works because it mirrors the exact search query a worried owner types. It doesn't pretend to be a "cat behavior guide" or a "feline wellness resource" — it's the question living in your head at 9 p.m. after getting your hand opened up by an animal you've loved for years. Curiosity-driven, zero judgment.

*Alternative:* "The Sudden Bite: A Cat Owner's Medical + Behavioral Field Guide" (57 chars — works better if subtitle is formatted below title)

---

## 2. Emotional Hook

Your cat has been the sweetest, easiest companion for months — maybe years. Then one day, without warning, she's latching onto your hand, your ankle, your face. Not a love bite. A real one. And now you're lying awake running through every recent interaction, wondering: *Is she sick? Did I do something wrong? Is she going to keep getting worse?*

Here's what most guides won't tell you: sudden aggression in a previously friendly cat is one of the most common and reliable signs that something is physically wrong. It's not bad behavior. It's not spite. It's a cat trying to communicate pain, confusion, or fear the only way she knows how.

This guide is for you. It's not a behavioral pep talk. It's a structured, step-by-step framework that walks you through the two tracks you need to run simultaneously — medical and environmental — so you can figure out what's actually going on, what you can handle at home, and when you need a veterinarian in the room. You'll leave with a concrete action plan, not another "be patient and observe."

## 3. Product Architecture: 4 Parts, 12 Chapters

---

### PART 1: THE FIRST 24 HOURS — WHAT TO DO RIGHT NOW

**Why this part first:** When someone's in fear and frustration, they don't need theory. They need a decision framework. This part gives them a triage tool they can use *tonight*.

---

**Chapter 1 — Stop the Escalation: Immediate Safety and De-escalation**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Use a 5-step shutdown protocol to safely end an aggressive episode without punishing the cat
- Identify the 3 most dangerous common mistakes owners make during a bite event (and what to do instead)
- Understand why reaching for, picking up, or verbally scolding an aggressive cat in that moment makes the next episode worse

*Concrete output:* Includes a printed **Aggression Episode Log** template — a single page they can start filling out immediately to track every incident with timestamp, context, body language observed, and outcome.

---

**Chapter 2 — Read the Body Language Before It Reads You**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Identify the 6 pre-bite warning signs most owners miss (hint: it's almost never just the bite)
- Differentiate between defensive aggression signals and offensive/prey-drive signals — because they need completely different responses
- Distinguish ambient stress signals (stuff owners dismiss as "quirks") from active escalation signals

*Concrete output:* A **Feline Stress-to-Bite Spectrum** one-page visual they can keep on the fridge — a working reference that replaces guesswork with pattern recognition.

---

**Chapter 3 — Is This a Medical Emergency or Not? The Triage Checklist**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Run themselves through a 10-question triage checklist to determine if they have a same-day veterinary urgency
- Understand why "acting normal between episodes" doesn't rule out a medical cause
- Recognize the 3 scenarios where waiting until Monday is genuinely dangerous

*Concrete output:* A **Veterinary Urgency Checklist** (portrait, printer-ready). Not a symptom list — an *action* checklist. Check the boxes that apply, call the vet if the score crosses threshold.

---

### PART 2: THE MEDICAL TRACK — WHAT YOUR VET MAY NOT TELL YOU

**Why this part first:** Most cat aggression guides go behavioral-only and miss the biggest cause. This part earns trust immediately because it validates the owner's gut feeling that something might be medically wrong — then gives them the tools to act on it.

---

**Chapter 4 — How to Talk to Your Vet So They Don't Dismiss the Biting**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Walk into a vet appointment with a structured 5-point agenda instead of starting with "she's been acting weird"
- Know which tests actually rule out medical causes of aggression (and which are expensive but irrelevant for this issue)
- Use the Aggression Episode Log from Part 1 as a clinical communication tool — giving the vet data instead of narrative

*Concrete output:* A **Veterinary Visit Prep Sheet** — the exact questions to ask, tests to request, and information to bring. Developed to feel like a script, not a lecture.

---

**Chapter 5 — The Medical Conditions Most Likely Behind Sudden Aggression**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Understand, in plain language, how hyperthyroidism, FHS, osteoarthritis, dental disease, and cognitive decline directly cause pain-driven aggression
- Know what questions to ask to screen for each condition specifically, without self-diagnosing
- Recognize when aggression is the *presenting symptom* of something more systemic

*Concrete output:* A **Condition Comparison Table** — side-by-side layout showing which conditions are common, which are painful, which are treatable, and what the behavioral pattern looks like. No medical jargon.

---

**Chapter 6 — The Pain Audit: How to Find What Your Cat Is Hiding**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Perform a gentle, structured home mobility check to identify joint pain, spinal tenderness, or oral discomfort
- Interpret the 7 most common behavioral shifts that indicate a cat is in chronic pain (not acute — the kind owners call "normal aging")
- Understand why cats hide pain and what "displacement behaviors" look like in the context of aggression

*Concrete output:* A **Home Pain Assessment Guide** — step-by-step instructions with illustrated tips on how to physically examine a reluctant cat without triggering a new bite event.

---

### PART 3: THE BEHAVIORAL TRACK — ENVIRONMENTAL AND EMOTIONAL CAUSES

**Why this part after medical:** Once medical causes are screened, the behavioral picture becomes clearer and less scary. This part is written for the owner who has already been to the vet or who has ruled out obvious medical causes — and still has a biting cat.

---

**Chapter 7 — Decoding the Type of Aggression: Fear, Play, Petting, or Redirected?**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Use a 4-category diagnostic framework to correctly identify which type of aggression is driving their cat's behavior
- Understand why "fear-based" and "play-based" require opposite interventions — and doing the wrong one makes things worse
- Apply the framework to their own Aggression Episode Log to find patterns without a behaviorist

*Concrete output:* A **Aggression Type Diagnostic Worksheet** — a fillable one-page tool where the owner maps their incidents to the four categories and determines the dominant type.

---

**Chapter 8 — The "Friendly to Feral" Profile: What Changed in Your Cat's Environment**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Identify the 8 most common environmental triggers for sudden behavioral aggression in indoor cats
- Specifically address the free-roaming-to-indoor transition scenario and why it causes face-biting (referencing the verbatim quote directly — this is a known, documented pattern)
- Recognize when the aggression is a grief response, a territorial reassertion, or a sensory confusion issue

*Concrete output:* An **Environmental Change Audit** — a checklist of 12 common household changes (new pet, move, schedule shift, construction noise, new furniture) and whether each is a likely contributor.

---

**Chapter 9 — The Litterbox–Aggression Connection Nobody Explains**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Understand the direct neurological pathway between litterbox avoidance/anxiety and redirected aggression
- Screen their litterbox setup for the 5 factors that cause chronic low-grade stress (which manifests as aggression)
- Know when a "behavior problem" is actually a bathroom infrastructure problem

*Concrete output:* A **Litterbox Audit Checklist** — covers box type, location, number, substrate, and cleaning schedule. Includes the minimum changes that reliably reduce ambient stress.

---

### PART 4: THE INTERVENTION TOOLKIT — WHAT ACTUALLY WORKS

**Why this part last:** You now know what's wrong. This part gives you what to do. Every chapter ends with a playbook, not a philosophy.

---

**Chapter 10 — Play Therapy That Actually Reduces Biting (Not Just Tires the Cat Out)**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Run a scientifically structured play session (type, duration, frequency, prey mimicry) that satisfies the hunting cycle — not just "wrestle a feather wand for five minutes"
- Implement the prey-sequence protocol for cats whose play aggression has become self-reinforcing
- Distinguish between "I'm bored" play and "I'm in pain and this is the only way to tell you" play

*Concrete output:* A **14-Day Play Therapy Schedule** — daily structure with session length, toy types, and escalation progression. Designed for working owners. No more than 15 minutes of active engagement per day.

---

**Chapter 11 — Biting-Specific Protocols for Face, Hands, and Ankle Ambushes**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Stop the "rough-hands = play invitation" association using a concrete household rule system (not just "don't use your hands")
- Implement a redirection protocol specifically for face-biting during close contact moments
- Use the "escape route" technique correctly — most owners do this wrong and it trains the cat to bite harder

*Concrete output:* A **Scenario-Specific Intervention Deck** — 5 bite scenarios (face during petting, hand ambush during movement, ankle grab mid-hallway, etc.) each with a 3-step protocol. Laminate-friendly.

---

**Chapter 12 — Building Trust After the Bite: Long-Term Relationship Repair**

*What the reader will be able to DO:*

- Implement a structured trust-rebuilding protocol for cats who've learned to expect threat from their owners
- Know exactly what "go-at-their-pace" looks like in practice — with a week-by-week progression
- Recognize when the relationship is genuinely improving versus when they're just managing symptoms

*Concrete output:* A **5-Week Trust Rebuilding Timeline** — a week-by-week visual roadmap with milestone markers for both the cat and the owner. Includes a "red flags vs. normal healing behaviors" reference section.
