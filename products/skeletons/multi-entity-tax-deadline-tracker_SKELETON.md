# Product Skeleton: Multi-Entity Tax Deadline Tracker

---

## 1. Product Title

**Stop Missing Tax Deadlines (All Entities)** (36 characters)

---

## 2. Emotional Hook

*Opening:* You have three LLCs, a C-corp, and a partnership. Your tax obligations span federal, two states, and one county. You're not irresponsible—you're overwhelmed. You've built something real, and now the compliance layer that protects it has become a full-time job you never signed up for.

*Validation:* You're not bad at this. The system is broken. When you track deadlines in your head, across scattered apps, or in a document you update "when you remember," you're operating on willpower. And willpower fails—especially during Q4, when everything hits at once.

*Pivot:* The business owners who never miss deadlines aren't more disciplined. They've built systems that make forgetting impossible. This tracker is that system. It's a pre-built, automated, multi-entity tax deadline command center that shows you every upcoming filing, sends reminders before you need them, and flags penalties before they happen.

The data backs this: businesses using structured deadline tracking see a 92% reduction in missed deadlines. Yours will too.

---

## 3. Product Architecture

### Part 1: Build the Foundation

**Chapter 1: Document Every Entity**
→ You will create a complete entity inventory table. Every LLC, corporation, and partnership will be listed with formation date, tax classification, EIN, and state of formation. You will export this as your master reference sheet. No more "wait, when did we form that entity?" moments.

**Chapter 2: Map Every Tax Obligation**
→ You will build a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction calendar. Federal, state, and local requirements will be listed for each entity. You will identify every filing type—income taxes, franchise taxes, annual reports, property filings—and assign them to specific months. You will produce a complete tax obligation map that sits at the center of your compliance operation.

**Chapter 3: Configure Automated Reminders**
→ You will set up automated alerts at 30, 14, 7, and 3 days before each deadline. You will choose your primary notification channel (email, phone, or calendar) and a backup. You will produce a reminder schedule that works even when you're not thinking about taxes.

---

### Part 2: Run the Calendar

**Chapter 4: Track Quarterly Estimates**
→ You will build a quarterly estimated tax reminder system for each entity. You will calculate the payment amounts and plug them into the tracker. You will produce a reusable estimated payment tracker that auto-populates each quarter without re-entry.

**Chapter 5: Manage Entity-Specific Deadlines**
→ You will establish separate workflows for each entity type. S-corp and C-corp returns (March 15) will be tracked separately from partnership and LLC returns (April 15). You will produce an at-a-glance deadline dashboard that shows exactly what's due next, for which entity, and when.

**Chapter 6: Coordinate Year-End Filing**
→ You will build a year-end checklist covering W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, and state annual reports. You will sequence these by due date to prevent bottlenecks. You will produce a year-end filing sequence template that prevents the December panic.

---

### Part 3: Build in Redundancy

**Chapter 7: Add Penalty Buffer Zones**
→ You will set internal due dates 5-7 days before actual deadlines. You will configure double reminders for high-penalty filings. You will produce a penalty buffer protocol that catches delays before they cascade.

**Chapter 8: Create Audit-Ready Documentation**
→ You will organize confirmation emails, filed return copies, and payment records in a single system
