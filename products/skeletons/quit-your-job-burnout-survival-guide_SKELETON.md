# Product Skeleton

**Trend:** How to quit strategically (Score: 10/12)
**Audience:** Workers who quit due to severe burnout — quitting without backup plan

---

## Product Title

**How to Quit Without Losing Everything**
(37 characters — grounded curiosity, not clickbait)

Alternate: *The Strategic Quit* (for brand-forward positioning)

---

## Emotional Hook

Your body already made the decision. You just haven't said it out loud yet.

You know that coworker who's "running on fumes" — that's you. Except it's not fumes anymore. It's something deeper. You're getting sick more often. Your chest tightens on Sunday nights. You've started dreading the commute before it even starts. And the worst part? You took this job to escape something worse, and somehow it still caught up with you.

You want to quit. Maybe you've already fantasized about it — the email drafted, the resignation speech rehearsed in the shower. But then the fear kicks in: the rent, the gap in your resume, the "what if I'm just not cut out for working."

Here's what nobody tells you: quitting without a plan isn't reckless. It's survival. And there's a real difference between burning out with no strategy and burning out with a plan to get yourself back.

This product gives you the exact blueprint for quitting in a way that protects your finances, your relationships, and your mental health — so when you walk out that door, you're not just escaping. You're stepping forward.

## Product Architecture

### Part 1: The Money Foundation

**Before you type a single resignation letter, you need to know where you stand.**

This part forces you to get real about your financial picture — not in a scary way, but in a clarifying way. Knowing your number changes everything.

---

**Chapter 1: Calculate Your True Monthly Burn**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Identify every fixed and variable expense currently in your life (including the ones you pretend don't exist, like streaming subscriptions and "occasional" takeout)
- Calculate your exact monthly survival number — the bare minimum you'd need to cover essentials
- Distinguish between "lifestyle spending" and "survival spending" so you can make honest cuts if needed

*Output: A completed "Burn Rate Worksheet" with your monthly survival number highlighted.*

---

**Chapter 2: Build a Quit-Quarter Runway**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Determine how many months of runway you need for a safe exit (most people underestimate by 2–3 months)
- Access your existing financial tools (emergency funds, side income, credit, family support) and weigh them honestly
- Create a "runway plan" that covers healthcare, housing, and basic sanity expenses for at least 90 days post-quit

*Output: A 90-day runway plan with specific dollar targets and funding sources.*

---

**Chapter 3: Access Resources You Didn't Know You Had**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Identify government and nonprofit resources available to the recently unemployed in your state/country (unemployment benefits, COBRA alternatives, healthcare subsidies)
- Apply for at least one financial bridge resource within 48 hours of quitting
- Build a "bridge income" plan — part-time, gig, or contract work you can activate immediately if runway runs short

*Output: A customized resource checklist with application links and deadlines for your specific situation.*

---

### Part 2: The Exit Frame

**Now it's time to actually leave. But not badly — strategically.**

This part gives you the exact scripts, timing, and moves for resigning without burning bridges you might need later. Yes, even if your company was toxic. Yes, even if you're furious.

---

**Chapter 4: The 72-Hour Exit Protocol**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Draft a resignation email that is professional, brief, and protects your future references (without being groveling)
- Know exactly what to do in your final 72 hours at work — files, contacts, passwords, and the conversations that matter
- Avoid the three most common exit mistakes that damage professional reputation and future employability

*Output: A completed resignation email draft ready to send, plus a 72-hour exit checklist.*

---

**Chapter 5: How to Answer "Why Are You Leaving?"**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Deliver a neutral-to-positive explanation of your departure that doesn't burn bridges or invite debate
- Redirect a counteroffer conversation without making enemies (even if you never wanted to stay anyway)
- Handle exit interviews in a way that protects your severance and benefits eligibility

*Output: A personalized "departure script" for your specific exit scenario — counteroffer, exit interview, or straight resignation.*

---

**Chapter 6: The Cleanup You Do Before You Go**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Audit your digital footprint (LinkedIn, professional profiles, email signature) so you're "officially" in a position to job search the moment you leave
- Secure professional references — specifically, ask at least two colleagues to be formal references while you're still on good terms
- Draft a brief, non-bitter "thank you" note to one person who made the job survivable (this matters more than you think)

*Output: A cleaned-up professional presence and a written reference request sent to at least two people.*

---

### Part 3: The Immediate Aftermath

**You quit. It's done. Now the real work begins — and it's not job searching.**

The weeks after quitting are when most people panic, spiral, and rush into the next wrong thing. This part keeps you grounded.

---

**Chapter 7: The First Two Weeks Are for Rest, Not Job Applications**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Recognize the physical and emotional recovery phase as necessary — not lazy — and set appropriate expectations with yourself and others
- Create a "no agenda" two-week period that actually lets your nervous system reset
- Identify the difference between productive rest and avoidance (and know when you're doing the latter)

*Output: A personalized two-week rest protocol with specific daily "do nothing" commitments.*

---

**Chapter 8: Rebuild Your Daily Structure From Scratch**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Replace work-driven routine with a self-designed structure that supports recovery and clear thinking
- Establish morning and evening routines that don't rely on "productivity" as a measure of worth
- Create a weekly rhythm that balances rest, job searching, and living like a human being

*Output: A 7-day structure template that can be customized and iterated — not a rigid plan, a flexible scaffold.*

---

**Chapter 9: Work Through the Guilt**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Identify the specific sources of your post-quit guilt (family expectations, financial anxiety, identity loss, fear of judgment)
- Use a structured "guilt audit" to separate legitimate concerns from internalized noise
- Develop a personal framework for making guilt-informed decisions without being paralyzed by fear

*Output: A completed "Guilt Audit Worksheet" with specific reframes for each major source of anxiety.*

---

### Part 4: The What Comes Next

**You're rested. You're clear. Now it's time to decide what actually comes next — on your terms.**

This part isn't about rushing into the next job. It's about making a deliberate choice about your next chapter that doesn't repeat the last one.

---

**Chapter 10: Honest Self-Assessment — Industry, Role, or Exit?**
*After this chapter, you will be able to:*

- Evaluate whether your burnout was caused by the specific company, the role type, or the entire industry
- Make a clear-eyed decision about whether to return to your field, pivot to a related role, or leave the sector entirely
- Avoid the "panic job search" that leads to accepting the same dynamic with a different employer

*Output: A completed "Career Path Assessment" with a clear directional recommendation and three alternative scenarios mapped.*
