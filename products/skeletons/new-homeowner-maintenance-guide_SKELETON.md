## PROBLEM DIAGNOSIS

New homeowners lack a year-one maintenance roadmap. They can't distinguish a $50 DIY fix from a $5,000 emergency call. The result: paralysis, expensive surprises, and preventable damage. Industry data confirms it — first-year costs average $86,698, and 29% of homeowners say delaying one repair led to a worse problem.

The root issue is **no mental model for triage.** Without one, every drip becomes an anxiety spiral and every invoice looks like a scam risk.
