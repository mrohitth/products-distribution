# Product Skeleton: Job Search Overhaul

---

## 1. Product Title (31 characters)

**Why You Keep Getting Passed Over**

*Curiosity-driven because it names the silent frustration without victim-blaming. Signals the system is the problem, not the person.*

---

## 2. Emotional Hook

You have a job. You've done the applications. You've practiced answers in the mirror. You've even started asking for raises—and your boss nods like something's happening, but nothing changes. You want out. But the interviews don't come, and the offers never land.

Here's what no one tells you: The problem probably isn't your competence. It's the gap between what you're doing in your job search and what the system actually rewards.

This isn't a motivation boost. This is a complete overhaul of your resume, interview conversion, and psychological infrastructure—built for someone who's tried the "just keep applying" approach and landed in desperation instead of an offer.

You'll leave this with a resume that passes automated screens, interview instincts that convert, and the internal steadiness to sustain a search without unraveling. By the time you're done, the rejection cycle breaks—not because the market suddenly loved you, but because you finally understand how the game actually works.

---

## 3. Product Architecture

### PART 1: The Resume That Stops Disappearing

**Chapter 1: Reverse-Engineer the ATS Before It Rejects You**

*The output:* A "keyword extraction checklist" they complete using 5 real job postings from companies they actually want. By documenting which terms appear in requirements versus job descriptions, they identify the exact language gap causing their resume to filter out before human eyes ever see it.

**Chapter 2: Rewrite Every Section From "Me" to "Them"**

*The output:* A before/after resume analysis. They rewrite their professional summary, experience bullets, and skills section using a proven formula: action verb + measurable outcome + keyword alignment. They'll have a completed revised resume template they can verify against ATS screening criteria.

**Chapter 3: Test It Before You Submit**

*The output:* A documented "ATS audit report"—a simple checklist confirming their resume passes formatting, keyword density, and structure thresholds before it goes out. No more submitting blind.

---

### PART 2: The Interview That Converts

**Chapter 4: Build a Modular Answer Architecture**

*The output:* A completed "story bank" containing 8-10 adaptable stories mapped to common question categories. They'll master the STAR method but also learn when to deviate from it—producing answers that feel natural rather than rehearsed. The concrete output is a printed reference card they can review before any interview.

**Chapter 5: Control the Room Before You Speak**

*The output:* A "first five minutes protocol" they practice and can execute without thinking. This includes specific body language resets, an opener that establishes warmth without desperation, and a technique to buy time on tough questions. They'll record themselves executing it and have a checklist to self-score before going live.

**Chapter 6: Turn Rejection Into Intelligence**

*The output:* A "rejection debrief template" they use after every interview—no matter the outcome. This ensures every experience feeds the next one. For post-rejection conversations (when possible), they'll have a specific question script that extracts actionable feedback most candidates never ask for.

---

### PART 3: The Psychological Resilience That Holds the Line

**Chapter 7: Isolate the Actual Problem**

*The output:* A diagnostic worksheet distinguishing between "skill gap" and "self-worth damage." Most job seekers conflate these, which makes everything feel hopeless. They'll identify exactly what needs fixing versus what needs grieving—and create a targeted action plan for each.

**Chapter 8: Build a Rebuttal Narrative They Can Actually Use**

*The output:* A "reframe document" they write and can deploy when rejection hits at 2am. This isn't positive thinking—it's a structured argument against their specific inner critic, backed by evidence from their actual track record. They'll have three documented rebuttals ready before the next rejection arrives.

**Chapter 9: Reset the Nervous System on Command**

*The output:* A portable "reset protocol"—a short, audio-guided somatic exercise (under 90 seconds) they can use in a bathroom stall before an interview or during a panic moment during a search. They'll have practiced it three times and know exactly when to deploy it. No abstraction; real nervous system regulation they can execute under pressure.

---

### PART 4: The System That Sustains the Search

**Chapter 10: Build a Week That Survives Rejection**

*The output:* A custom "search architecture"—a documented weekly schedule allocating specific hours to applications, interviews, and recovery. They'll create accountability structures (even solo ones) and a protocol for what happens when silence stretches past day seven. The goal is sustainable effort disconnected from daily outcome anxiety.

**Chapter 11: Identify the Sabotage Patterns You're Running**

*The output:* A self-audit identifying their top 3 job search self-sabotage behaviors. Common patterns include over-researching companies as avoidance, underpricing themselves preemptively, and ghosting applications when they "don't feel like it." They'll map triggers to behaviors and build a contingency plan for each.

**Chapter 12: The Complete Job Search Playbook (Final Integration)**

*The output:* A single-page "war room" reference sheet consolidating their ATS-optimized resume, story bank, answer frameworks, interview protocol, reset techniques, and search schedule into one document. They'll have a laminated or printed reference they can consult in under 30 seconds—eliminating the mental overwhelm that compounds with every rejection.

## 4. Key Human Touch Elements

**The "I Ask For Raises But Nothing Happens" Frame**

The verbatim quote reveals someone with enough professional standing to request compensation adjustments. That's not the behavior of someone incompetent—that's someone who believes in their value but lacks leverage with their current employer. This distinction is the emotional pivot: this product doesn't rebuild confidence from zero; it redirects existing confidence into a system that works.

**The ATS Truth Bomb (Early Placement)**

The ATS system filters out 70-80% of resumes before human eyes. Most job seekers interpret silence as rejection of their worth. Early in the product, this must be explicitly named and documented—not to depress them, but to surgically remove guilt from the equation. Every reader who has blamed themselves for silence will exhale at this point.

**The "I'm Bad at Interviews" Reframe**

This belief often persists after coaching because it hasn't been properly challenged. We introduce a distinction: "You don't yet have a system for interviews that converts" versus "You are inherently bad at interviews." One is fixable. The product teaches the system.

**The Somatic Anchor**

Psychological advice often feels like self-help abstraction. Including a concrete, 90-second somatic reset (backed by nervous system science) gives readers something tactile, testable, and real. Most will share this with others—which becomes organic word-of-mouth.

## 5. Format & Pricing

**Format:**

- Main product: PDF guide (120-140 pages) with embedded worksheets
- Companion: Printable workbook containing all chapter exercise templates
- Supplementary: Audio files for somatic reset exercises (3 tracks, under 5 minutes total)
- Tracker: Spreadsheet template for application pipeline and interview debriefs

**Price Point Rationale:**

$79 — Positioned above impulse-buy territory ($19-29 range, which signals low-stakes content) and below professional coaching ($500+). This is someone who has spent months, maybe years, stuck in a cycle that costs them salary, sanity, and opportunity. $79 represents two hours of coaching at average rates—anchored value. The audience is desperate enough to invest but has been burned by generic advice, so perceived quality must signal depth.

**Rationale:** The worksheets and tracker make it feel like a workbook, not a book. The audio files add physiological credibility. The total deliverable weight justifies three-digit pricing without shocking a market that knows job search pain firsthand.
