# Product Skeleton: Freelancer Legal Protection

---

## 1. Product Title

**Get Paid: The Freelancer's Legal Shield**
*(39 characters)*

---

## 2. Emotional Hook

Let's be honest: most freelancers learn the hard way. The client who ghosts after delivery. The one who disputes the scope after you've already spent 40 hours on revisions. The verbal agreement that meant nothing when it came time to pay. You've probably felt that specific frustration — the one where you did the work, you both knew what it was worth, and somehow you're the one left holding the loss while they're already on to their next project.

This isn't about being paranoid. It's about building infrastructure that makes getting burned the exception rather than the rule.

*Get Paid: The Freelancer's Legal Shield* doesn't teach you to be combative. It teaches you to be so organized, so clear, and so documented that a payment dispute becomes a solved problem rather than an existential crisis. You'll learn how to lock in agreements before the work starts, how to invoice in ways that actually hold up legally, and exactly what to do when a client goes dark. By the time you're reading this, you'll have the receipts — literal receipts — and a plan that doesn't require you to choose between getting paid and burning a bridge.

## 3. Product Architecture

### Part 1: Know Your Exposure (The Freelancer's Legal Reality)

**Chapter 1: Map Every Place a Client Can Screw You**
*Output: You will be able to identify and document the four high-risk interaction points in your client workflow — scope agreements, change orders, payment terms, and delivery confirmations — and create a simple risk matrix for your specific freelance type.*

**Chapter 2: Understand What "Verbal Agreements" Are Actually Worth**
*Output: You will be able to explain in plain language why verbal commitments are legally weak, what minimum written evidence looks like in your jurisdiction, and how to convert existing client relationships into documented ones without raising suspicion.*

**Chapter 3: Build Your Evidence File Before You Need It**
*Output: You will set up a chronological project folder system (email threads, approvals, deliverables, payment records) that can be exported as a complete documentation package in under 10 minutes if a dispute arises.*

---

### Part 2: Contract With Confidence (The Agreement Framework)

**Chapter 4: Write a Scope of Work That Doesn't Leave You Exposed**
*Output: You will draft a SOW template with explicit deliverables, exclusions, revision limits, and acceptance criteria — language that gives you leverage when clients expand scope mid-project without agreeing to additional compensation.*

**Chapter 5: Add the Six Clauses That Actually Protect You**
*Output: You will be able to identify and insert into any contract: (1) payment terms and late fee structure, (2) kill fee for early termination, (3) IP transfer upon full payment, (4) dispute resolution method, (5) limitation of liability cap, and (6) right to pause work for non-payment.*

**Chapter 6: Make Signing Feel Normal, Not Suspicious**
*Output: You will implement a low-friction contract workflow using free tools (Google Docs comments, DocuSign free tier, or even email acknowledgment) that normalizes the signing process so it's not a relationship-killing moment.*

---

### Part 3: Invoice Like You Mean It (The Payment Mechanics)

**Chapter 7: Write Invoices That Are Legally Binding Receipts**
*Output: You will create an invoice template that includes all legally significant elements — description of services, agreed amount, payment due date, accepted payment methods, and explicit language tying deliverables to payment — and understand why each element matters.*

**Chapter 8: Structure Payments So You're Never Out-of-Pocket**
*Output: You will design a payment schedule (deposit + milestone + final) for your services that limits your exposure to any single invoice to an amount you're willing to absorb, and negotiate this structure with new clients.*

**Chapter 9: Handle Late Payments Without Losing Your Mind**
*Output: You will implement a systematic late payment escalation process — friendly reminder at day 3, firm reminder at day 7, payment-on-hold notice at day 10, final notice at day 14 — that preserves the relationship while protecting your leverage.*

---

### Part 4: Collect Without Burning Bridges (The Recovery Playbook)

**Chapter 10: Know Exactly What "Written Evidence" Means for Your Case**
*Output: You will be able to assemble a complete evidence package — emails, contract, signed SOW, invoice history, delivery confirmations, payment records — formatted in a way that strengthens your position in any formal dispute process.*

**Chapter 11: Understand Your Legal Recourse Options (Without Hiring a Lawyer)**
*Output: You will be able to list the three realistic escalation paths available to you: formal demand letter (free, self-serve), small claims court process (filing cost ~$30-$150, no lawyer required), and statutory demand for incorporated clients — including estimated timelines and success probability for each.*

**Chapter 12: Decide When to Fight and When to Walk Away**
*Output: You will apply a simple cost-benefit decision framework to unpaid invoices: calculate the true cost of pursuit (time, emotional load, legal fees) against the amount owed, your relationship value, and the probability of recovery — and make a clear, documented decision you can stand behind.*

## 4. Key Human Touch Elements

**The Shame Anchor**
Freelancers don't just lose money — they feel *stupid* for trusting. They beat themselves up for not getting a signed contract, for not following their gut when the client went quiet. This product needs to validate that the mistake was systemic, not personal. Framing: "The client who doesn't want to sign anything is telling you something. Now you have a way to hear it without walking away from revenue."

**The Bridge Myth**
The biggest reason freelancers don't pursue payment: fear of looking difficult and losing future work. The guide should explicitly name this fear and reframe it. Data point to include: "Clients who don't pay you aren't giving you future work anyway." Humanize it with the anecdote of a freelancer who spent three months agonizing over a $800 invoice, eventually got paid after a five-minute formal demand, and the client referred two more projects.

**The "Average Freelancer Gets Burned" Stat**
Include: ~70% of freelancers experience non-payment at least once. Average amount: $1,200-$3,400 depending on industry. This normalizes the problem and makes the investment in prevention feel rational rather than paranoid.

**The "How Did This Happen?" Story**
Open Part 2 with a brief composite story: the freelancer who did everything right on the project and nothing right on the paperwork. Three paragraphs. Don't editorialize. Just walk through the sequence — verbal yes, 80 hours of work, scope creep, no payment, no recourse. End with: "This was preventable." Then walk through what was missing.

## 5. Format & Pricing

**Format:** PDF download with:
- 12 fillable templates (contract clauses, SOW framework, invoice template, late payment email sequences, evidence folder checklist)
- One-page quick-reference card (the "When They Don't Pay" flowchart)
- Appendix: State-by-state small claims court filing basics (or jurisdiction-neutral guide)

**Pricing Rationale:** $27 is the sweet spot for standalone guides in this category. It's low enough to feel like a no-brainer for freelancers who've already lost $1,000+ to one bad client. It's high enough to signal quality and commitment. Test at $19 for launch, $27 at scale — the difference in perceived value at $19 is meaningful for impulse buyers who need this *today*.
