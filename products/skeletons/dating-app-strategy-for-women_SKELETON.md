# Product Skeleton: The Intentional Dater

**Format:** PDF + 6 printable templates
**Audience:** Women 28–35 | On apps 1–3 years | Nearing or in burnout
**Conviction:** High — exhaustion is real, and the gap between "trying hard" and "trying strategically" is wide and underserved.

---

## 1. Product Title

**"The Intentional Dater"** (18 chars)

**Why this title:** "Intentional" signals agency and a framework, not just another attitude shift. It's not preachy — it reads like a working method, not a pep talk. It also does something important: it separates this reader from people who approach dating casually, which she is not. That distinction lands immediately.

---

## 2. Emotional Hook

The exhaustion you're feeling after that "perfect guy" didn't text back — that's not a you problem. That's a system problem.

Dating apps were built to keep you scrolling, not to help you find a partner. Every new match feels like a small hit of hope, and every silence feels like a small death. You're not broken. You're not too picky or too demanding or too old. You've just been playing a game with rules that were designed against your interest, and nobody told you that was the case.

The Intentional Dater isn't about becoming someone else, learning "the trick," or fixing your profile for more attention. It's about having a real framework so that when you do engage with these apps — which you'll probably keep doing because they're the actual infrastructure available to you — you do it from a place of control. You get to decide when you swipe, how much attention you give it, and who you actually invest time in. The goal isn't to be constantly on. The goal is to protect your energy, vet more efficiently, and be genuinely ready when the right person appears.

You will learn to stop making decisions from a place of scarcity. And you will learn to make them faster.

## 3. Product Architecture

### Part 1: The Mental Reset — Reclaim Your Baseline Energy

**Chapter 1: Reframe Dating as Energy Management, Not Luck**
You will audit how dating apps currently drain you — specific patterns like compulsive post-swipe checking, over-investing in early conversations before they're earned, and absorbing the emotional weight of silence. You'll identify your personal burnout triggers with a structured reflection worksheet. Output: a personal "dating energy audit" — a one-page document that maps exactly where your time and emotional bandwidth are going right now.

**Chapter 2: Set Criteria Before You Swipe — Not After You're Hooked**
You will build a "must-haves" and "deal-breakers" list that actually functions as a filter, not a wishlist. This means distinguishing between what sounds good in theory and what you genuinely cannot compromise on. You'll practice writing criteria that are specific enough to act on and realistic enough not to eliminate everyone. Output: a completed "Match Criteria Card" — printable template with three must-haves, three non-negotiables, and a clear decision rule for edge cases.

**Chapter 3: Define What "Active" Looks Like on Your Terms**
You will design a sustainable engagement schedule — not a strategy to "maximize volume," but a structure that keeps you present without treating the apps like a second job. This includes setting swipe windows (e.g., two sessions per week, 20 minutes max), deciding when to respond to messages, and creating hard rules about what happens after a date. Output: a completed "Active Mode Settings" document — a personal protocol you can actually stick to.

---

### Part 2: The Filter — Stop Matching with People Who Waste Your Time

**Chapter 4: Reading Bios and Prompts Like a Data Analyst, Not a Dreamer**
You will learn to extract behavioral signals from dating profiles — what someone actually writes, not just how they look. You'll identify patterns that predict low effort (vague prompts, deflection humor, zero specific interests), medium investment, and high intent. You'll practice the distinction between someone who is open to connection versus someone who is performing openness. Output: a completed "Profile Read Checklist" — a 10-item guide you run on every profile before you swipe.

**Chapter 5: The First Three Messages — What to Look For and What to Exit**
You will learn to identify conversation quality in the first exchange — whether someone asks questions that show genuine interest or just reacts, whether they sustain a thread or drop it after one reply, whether they demonstrate basic conversational reciprocity. You'll have a clear exit protocol: what combination of signals tells you this conversation will not become a date worth having, and how to end it without guilt or explanation. Output: a "Conversation Exit Decision Tree" — a printable flowchart with three decision nodes and concrete example scripts for graceful exits.

**Chapter 6: How to Move Off the App in Two Days (Or Less)**
You will learn the mechanics of transitioning to a phone/video call or a date, with specific language that communicates intent without coming on too strong. You'll understand why conversations that stay on the app for longer than 48 hours almost always degrade — and what to do about it. You'll have a decision rubric for whether to text, call, or set a date based on conversation quality, not emotional investment. Output: a "Transition Script Library" — five concrete message templates for different conversation tones, all low-pressure and clearly intentional.

---

### Part 3: The Evaluation — Know Who Is Actually Worth Your Time

**Chapter 7: Red Flags Before the Date — How to Catch Them in Writing**
You will learn to identify the behavioral patterns that precede incompatibility: future-faking language ("we should do this together sometime"), love-bombing cadence (excessive enthusiasm before a real foundation exists), vagueness about life circumstances, and pattern-based inconsistency in how they communicate. You'll build the skill of reading what's unsaid — not just what's written. Output: a completed "Pre-Date Signal Assessment" — a worksheet where you score a match on five behavioral indicators before you meet them.

**Chapter 8: Green Flags That Actually Predict Compatibility (Not Just Politeness)**
You will learn what genuine interest looks like in a text conversation — consistency of engagement, willingness to get slightly personal, ability to hold a thread across several days without you carrying it. You'll understand the difference between someone who is attracted to you and someone who is genuinely curious about you, and why that distinction matters enormously for long-term fit. Output: a "Green Flag Reference Card" — printable quick-reference for evaluating matches against five concrete behavioral markers.

**Chapter 9: The Pre-Date Calibration — What to Ask and What to Listen For**
You will learn a short list of conversation moves that reveal compatibility on key dimensions — lifestyle cadence, relationship expectations, relationship history, and energy toward the process itself — without it feeling like an interrogation. You'll practice phrasing that elicits real answers, not performance. Output: a "Pre-Date Talking Points" card — organized by topic, with three questions per dimension, plus guidance on how to read the answers in real time.

---

### Part 4: The Date — Execute With Intention, Exit With Clarity

**Chapter 10: The First Date as Information Gathering, Not Performance**
You will reframe the first date entirely — not as a test you can pass or fail, but as a structured data collection session. You'll prepare two to three genuine questions that matter to you specifically, understand the "one-drink boundary" (a first date capped at one drink or one hour), and know exactly how to end it without awkward over-explanation. Output: a "First Date Blueprint" — a printable prep card with sections for your top three questions, your exit line, and your post-date debrief trigger.

**Chapter 11: Post-Date Processing Without Spiraling**
You will learn a structured method for processing a first date without catastrophizing or romanticizing. This includes a 24-hour rule (no texting overhaul in the moment), a specific debrief format that separates your ego from your assessment, and how to identify genuine connection signals versus social chemistry. You'll also learn the clear criteria for when to reach out for a second date and when to let it sit. Output: a "Post-Date Debrief Worksheet" — a structured form to complete within 24 hours of every first date, covering behavioral observations, emotional response, and a decision recommendation.

**Chapter 12: The Energy Audit After Every Five Dates**
You will build a tracking habit that lets you see patterns across your dating history — not just who you liked, but who made you feel energetic versus depleted, who you were performing for versus showing up as yourself with. This is the system that prevents you from repeating the same mismatch across different faces. Output: a "Five-Date Review Template" — a quarterly reset form that helps you identify what's working, what pattern is repeating, and whether your criteria need adjustment.

## 4. Key Human Touch Elements

**The primary emotional entry point is this:** she has seen a glimpse of what the right person actually feels like — and that makes everything else feel like a letdown. Any messaging that dismisses or romanticizes that moment will lose her. Lean into the fact that this product doesn't shame her for being hopeful. It validates that she *knows* what she wants. The system exists because she's already doing the emotional work; she just needs infrastructure to match it.
