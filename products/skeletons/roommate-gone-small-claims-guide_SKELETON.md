# Product Skeleton

**Trend:** Small claims prep for roommate debt | **Conviction Score:** 11/12

## 1. Product Title

**The Roommate Payback Playbook**

*35 characters. Direct without being aggressive. "Payback" lands the emotional note of "this isn't okay and you have options" without the clickbait energy of "UNLOCK YOUR JUSTICE" or whatever. Signals that this is a practical, usable guide — not a legal textbook.*

---

## 2. Emotional Hook

You've been there: the moment you realize your roommate isn't just broke — they're weaponizing nonpayment. She makes more than you, moved in with mom, and has zero reason to be broke. She just hates your other roommate (her ex) and decided you both can go screw. Meanwhile, her stuff covers the couch you can't use, and asking for rent feels like pulling teeth from someone who spits in your direction.

You're not crazy for wanting this resolved. A spite-fueled nonpayer isn't a personal problem — it's a financial injury with a legal pathway. And small claims court exists precisely for situations where one person owes another money and won't pay.

This guide doesn't give you legal advice — it gives you a playbook. You'll know exactly what documentation to gather, how to calculate what you're owed (down to the dollar), how to file without a lawyer, and what happens when you show up to court. The answer isn't venting. It's winning.

---

## 3. Product Architecture

### Part 1: Know What You're Owed (And Can Actually Collect)

**Chapter 1: Reconstruct the Original Agreement**
- *Content:* Identifying written evidence (texts, emails, Venmo transactions with memos) versus oral agreements; how courts treat informal roommate arrangements; documenting who agreed to what, when, and under what terms
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a documented summary of the original financial agreement between them and the defendant, ready to present as the foundation of their claim

**Chapter 2: Calculate Your Actual Damages**
- *Content:* Rent and utilities owed, prorated calculations for mid-month moves, itemized damage claims (including the unusable common space), calculating prejudgment interest where applicable, what you can and cannot claim
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a complete damages worksheet with a specific dollar amount they're claiming, with each line item explained and legally justified

**Chapter 3: Confirm Your Legal Standing**
- *Content:* Small claims jurisdiction thresholds by state/city, verifying you're named as plaintiff correctly, lease interpretation (are you on the lease? subletting? who owes whom?), statute of limitations for written vs. verbal agreements
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have confirmed which specific court to file in and whether they have standing to sue, eliminating filing errors that cause dismissals

---

### Part 2: Evidence Collection Playbook

**Chapter 4: Audit Your Communication History**
- *Content:* Exporting and preserving text threads, email chains, Venmo/PayPal notes (screenshots with timestamps), how to authenticate digital evidence, organizing chronological records for court presentation
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a complete, timestamped evidence file of all financial communications, formatted to be admissible and easily referenced during court

**Chapter 5: Document the Shared Space Claim**
- *Content:* Photographing evidence of unusable common areas, calculating fair use value of blocked space, connecting the property claim to financial damages, third-party witness identification
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have photographic evidence catalogued with dates showing the defendant's belongings blocking common areas, linked to a specific dollar value of lost use

**Chapter 6: Pull Financial Records**
- *Content:* Bank statement review for payment patterns, Venmo/Zelle/PayPal transaction history, lease and utility payment records, creating a timeline of who paid what when
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a financial timeline showing the payment history and the specific point when payments stopped, establishing a clear breach pattern

---

### Part 3: Filing and Courtroom Strategy

**Chapter 7: File Without a Lawyer**
- *Content:* Step-by-step filing procedures by jurisdiction, filing fee payment (and how to include it in damages), serving the defendant properly, what to expect at filing, how to request a continuance if needed
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a completed, filed small claims complaint and proof of service, ready for their court date with zero attorney involvement

**Chapter 8: The Spite Defense — And How to Break It**
- *Content:* Anticipating the "I don't owe you because you did [X]" counterargument, using the defendant's own spite as evidence of intentional nonpayment, how to reframe personal conflict as a business dispute in court
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have prepared responses to every likely deflection the defendant will use, framed as evidence of bad faith rather than legitimate dispute

**Chapter 9: Your Day in Court**
- *Content:* The 3-minute opening statement structure, how to present evidence systematically, what judges actually ask (and what they're looking for), body language and composure basics, what to say when the defendant shows up angry
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a practiced, confident courtroom presentation that tells a clear story — not just presents evidence, but narrates what happened and why they're owed

---

### Part 4: After the Verdict

**Chapter 10: Collecting What You Won**
- *Content:* Understanding the judgment, wage garnishment procedures, bank levy process, property liens, how to find hidden assets, what to do when the defendant claims they "can't pay"
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a post-judgment collection plan with specific legal mechanisms to enforce the ruling, understanding that a win is Step 1, not the finish line

**Chapter 11: Handling the Appeal Window**
- *Content:* Understanding appeal deadlines (typically 10–30 days), what grounds defendants typically use, how to protect your judgment during the appeal period, collecting while an appeal is pending
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a timeline noting the appeal window closure date and know exactly what actions to take to secure their judgment before it can be challenged

**Chapter 12: Exit Clean — Document Everything**
- *Content:* Final demand letter with payment terms, what to say if they offer a partial payment, settlement agreement structure, what to do if they come back with a countersuit threat
- *Concrete output:* Reader will have a documented resolution (payment received, settlement signed, or enforcement initiated) with a paper trail that prevents future disputes
