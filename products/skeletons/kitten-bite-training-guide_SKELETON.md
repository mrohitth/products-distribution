## 1. Product Title

**Why Is My Kitten Attacking Me?**

## 2. Emotional Hook

*[Opening — pain validation]*
You've seen it. That moment when your kitten is a perfect warm weight in your lap, purring like a small motor. Your heart melts. And then—wham. Teeth on hand, claws out, full attack mode.

Nine times out of ten, when he's awake, he just constantly attacks your hands. You've got the scratches to prove it. You're starting to flinch when he approaches. You're wondering if this is just… your life now.

*[Pivot]*
Here's what you need to hear: this is normal kitten behavior. And it's fixable. Kittens bite because they're hardwired for hunting—and because they have a lot of energy with zero outlet. Every time your ankles move under the blanket, your kitten's prey drive kicks in.

This guide gives you a step-by-step system for stopping the biting at the source. You'll run a 5-minute energy audit to find the gap in your kitten's schedule. You'll learn the hunting sequence game that drains energy faster than anything else. You'll get three redirection techniques you can use mid-bite. And you'll set up a play schedule that makes biting unnecessary.

By the end, you'll have a kitten who still has plenty of drive—but has learned exactly where teeth and claws are allowed.

## 3. Product Architecture

### Part 1: Decode the Biting

**Chapter 1 — Identify the 5 bite triggers**
You will be able to recognize and name the five distinct situations that cause your kitten to bite (overstimulated play, predatory sequence activation, fear response, territorial guarding, attention-seeking). You will complete a 10-question diagnostic worksheet and leave knowing your kitten's primary trigger type. You will stop guessing and start responding to the actual cause.

**Chapter 2 — Map your kitten's energy cycle**
You will be able to plot your kitten's natural high-energy and low-energy periods across a 24-hour window. You will identify the specific times of day when biting is most likely to occur. You will see, for the first time, the pattern between unmet play needs and attack behavior—because it is always a pattern.

**Chapter 3 — Understand why punishment makes it worse**
You will understand the neuroscience behind why yelling, flicking, or squinting at your kitten doesn't reduce biting—it increases it by adding adrenaline and escalating arousal. You will stop three behaviors you've probably been doing that accidentally reinforce the cycle. You will feel less guilty about not being "firm enough."

---

### Part 2: Bite Inhibition Protocol

**Chapter 4 — Use the yelping technique correctly**
You will be able to replicate the exact high-pitched "eek!" sound that mimics how other cats communicate pain during rough play. You will practice the timing window (within 0.5 seconds of the bite) so your response registers as consequence, not play. You will understand why the yelp must be your *only* response and what to do with your hands afterward.

**Chapter 5 — Teach the soft-mouth via graduated withdrawal**
You will be able to run a 3-stage training progression: bites with pressure → bites without pressure → teeth-on-skin with no pressure. You will learn the two-week timeline for each stage and how to recognize when your kitten is ready to advance. You will have a kitten who can touch your skin without clamping down.

**Chapter 6 — Fade out the yelp over time**
You will be able to reduce yelp frequency as your kitten's soft-mouth solidifies, using a 5-day fading protocol. You will recognize the difference between a kitten testing boundaries versus reverting to old habits. You will have a clear exit strategy from the yelping system so you're not doing it forever.

---

### Part 3: Redirection & Play Schedule

**Chapter 7 — Three redirection techniques for mid-bite moments**
You will be able to execute three specific redirection moves: the toy toss (diverting attention mid-lunge), the motion freeze (breaking the predatory sequence), and the spatial swap (repositioning yourself as non-target). You will practice each one so redirection becomes muscle memory during the actual chaos of a bite event. You will stop flailing your hands, which is the single most common mistake new owners make.

**Chapter 8 — Structure an effective hunting sequence game**
You will be able to run a 10-minute interactive play session that follows the prey sequence—hunt, chase, pounce, catch, rest—because this is what actually tires out a kitten's brain. You will know the three wand toy techniques that maximize predatory engagement. You will stop defaulting to laser pointers (which cats cannot complete the catch sequence on, leading to frustration and anxiety).

**Chapter 9 — Build a weekly play schedule from scratch**
You will be able to write out a 7-day play schedule that spaces interactive sessions to match your kitten's energy peaks. You will leave with a filled-in template that's ready to tape to your fridge. You will know exactly what "enough play" looks like in minutes per day—and why most owners are underestimating by half.

---

### Part 4: Energy Outlet & Life Integration

**Chapter 10 — Create a kitten enrichment zone**
You will be able to set up a single designated play area with three types of enrichment (climbing, scratching, hiding) that gives your kitten a target-rich environment. You will reduce your own body being the default enrichment source—because right now, it is. You will have a space that burns energy even when you're not actively playing.

**Chapter 11 — Handle setbacks without starting over**
You will be able to distinguish between a normal regression and a true behavioral problem requiring a vet visit. You will have a three-step reset protocol for when your kitten bites after a calm week. You will stop catastrophizing normal developmental dips and stop abandoning the training because "it stopped working."

**Chapter 12 — Make it stick past 6 months**
You will be able to transition from active training mode to maintenance mode without losing progress. You will understand how adolescent cats (6–12 months) can regress and why this is not your fault. You will have a maintenance checklist that takes 5 minutes per week to audit. You will have a kitten who became an adult cat you actually enjoy living with.

## 4. Key Human Touch Elements

**Personal anecdote to open with:**
Include a short first-person account of a real kitten—like a reader's own situation. "My foster kitten, Mochi, drew blood on day two. I called the shelter thinking I had a problem cat. They laughed. A week later, Mochi was a different animal." This grounds the guide in lived experience and signals: the author gets it. They've been in the trenches with teeth on their hands.

**Validate the overwhelm, not just the bite:**
The emotional hook should name the specific exhaustion of flinching, of bleeding, of wondering if you'll ever just sit on the couch without vigilance. Many owners feel embarrassed that they "can't handle" a kitten—this is a guilt trap. The human touch should explicitly say: *this is a skill gap, not a character flaw. And skills are learnable.*

**Normalize the timeline:**
Frame it honestly: real reduction in 2–3 weeks, solid bite inhibition by 4–6 months, stable adult behavior by 12 months. Don't promise overnight miracles. The owners who succeed are the ones who know this is a marathon, not a sprint—and they're more likely to finish if you told them the course length upfront.

**Name the common failure points:**
Include a "things that feel like progress but aren't" section. Yelping inconsistently. Redirecting with the same hand that was just bitten. Ending play sessions without the catch-and-rest phase. These are the moments that挫折 people—the guide should name them so owners recognize the pattern and don't spiral.

## 5. Format & Pricing

**Format:** PDF guide + 3 printable worksheets
**Worksheets:** Energy Cycle Tracker, Weekly Play Schedule Template, Bite Trigger Diagnostic

**Price rationale:**
$17–$27 is the sweet spot for a niche digital guide with a single strong pain point and no fluff. Below $12, the guide feels like a throwaway blog post. Above $37, buyers expect video content, community, or ongoing coaching. At this price, you're selling a complete, actionable PDF with everything a reader needs—no upsell required. The printable worksheets justify the price point by making the guide interactive rather than passive.
